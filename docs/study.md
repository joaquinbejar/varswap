Outage regime (assumed, not historical: Hermes and Benchmarks need an API key, `docs/PLAN.md` verified facts): exogenous outages start at rate 1 per 168 h with an exponential length of mean 1 h. Halt models add endogenous halts: an hourly move above 3 σ_h halts the feed during the move (from inside that hour, across the boundary that ends it, then an exponential time of mean 2 h), and during the halt the latent price drifts 1 σ_h per hour with the move (continuation) or back against it (reversal); the move itself is inside the compressed return.
